How to download on iPhone

Save Instagram photos and videos on iPhone or iPad using Safari.

  1. Open Instagram, find the post, tap the share icon and choose Copy link.
  2. Open Safari and go to Reelinsta.
  3. Paste the link into the box, tick the permission box and tap Download.
  4. Pick a quality and tap the download button. When Safari asks, tap Download again.
  5. Open the Files app, then Downloads, to find your file. Tap it, then the share icon, then Save Video to put it in Photos.

Tip: if the download does not start, update iOS or try the free Documents by Readdle browser.
