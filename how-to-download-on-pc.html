How to download on PC

Save Instagram photos and videos on Windows, Mac or Linux.

  1. Open Instagram in your browser and open the post.
  2. Copy the address from the browser bar.
  3. Open Reelinsta in a new tab and paste the link.
  4. Tick the permission box and click Download.
  5. Click a quality. If the file opens in the browser instead, right-click it and choose Save video as.

Tip: your browser's download list (Ctrl+J on Windows, Cmd+Shift+J on Mac) shows where the file went.
