About the instagram story downloader

Stories disappear after 24 hours, so save yours in time. Paste the story link, which looks like instagram.com/stories/username/number.

Everything runs in your browser. No installation and no login are needed.

How to use it

  1. Open Instagram in the app or in your browser.
  2. Find the post, Reel, story or profile you want, and copy its link.
  3. Come back to Reelinsta and paste the link into the box.
  4. Tick the permission box and press Download.
  5. Choose a quality from the list and save the file to your device.

Questions

Do you keep a copy of my downloads?

No. We do not store the media you download, and we do not keep a history of the links you enter.

Why can I not download from some accounts?

Reelinsta only works with public posts. If an account is private, or the owner has limited sharing, we cannot reach the content and we will not try to.

Do I have to log in to Instagram?

No. Reelinsta never asks for your Instagram username or password.

Which quality will I get?

You get the best quality the original file offers. We show HD or 1080p when they exist, and a 4K choice appears only when the original upload is 4K.
