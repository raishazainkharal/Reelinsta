How to download on Android

Save Instagram photos and videos on an Android phone or tablet.

  1. Open Instagram, find the post and tap the three dots, then Copy link.
  2. Open Chrome and go to Reelinsta.
  3. Paste the link, tick the permission box and tap Download.
  4. Choose a quality and tap its button.
  5. Open your Files app, then Downloads, or your Gallery, to watch it.

Tip: if Chrome blocks the download, allow downloads for this site in Chrome settings.
